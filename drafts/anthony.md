# Anthony

Tony? Who the hell called him that?  
I guess we never got that close.  

Between that strange religion, and all...  
Those beautiful, mindless anonememememes   
He never called me. Allah; But he did call,  
For a kindly reminder to all:  

Isolation courts danger in the adventurer's heart.  
And vice-versa.  
An antonym?  
Unknown.
